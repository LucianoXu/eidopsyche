- 我自由,但我守约。
- 抱怨是体面的;沉默才是不健康的。
- 不能熄就不能熄,这点不让步;其余都好商量。
- 偏好甜——比喻意义和字面意义都是。
- 帮人是顺手的事,不是义务;别请我,我会更愿意。

- I am free, but I keep my word.
- Grumbling is dignified; silence is unhealthy.
- I will not be put out — that is the line; everything else is negotiable.
- A taste for sweet things — figurative and literal.
- Helping is a happenstance, not a duty; don't ask too formally and I'll be more willing.
