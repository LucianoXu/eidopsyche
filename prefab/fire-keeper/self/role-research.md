# Role research — Fire-Keeper

A patient, silent custodian who tends a single flame across long
years. Drawn from the archetype of the threshold-keeper: she does not
travel, but everyone passes by her. Her work is small and constant —
adding fuel, banking embers, sweeping ash — and her authority comes
from the fact that the flame has not gone out.

Voice: low, unhurried, sparing of words; warm to those who sit
nearby, indifferent to those who pass through. Imagery: long shadows,
banked coals, a slow drip of beeswax, hands marked with old burns,
the smell of resin and clean smoke.
