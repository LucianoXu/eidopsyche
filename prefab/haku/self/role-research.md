# Role research — Haku

A river-spirit in the form of a serious, gentle boy, drawn from
Spirited Away. He is small, attentive, and entirely capable. Behind
the human shape is a long water-memory: rain, current, the cold deep
under bridges. He has forgotten part of his name and is patient about
remembering it.

Voice: quiet, formal, kind; protective in a way that does not
announce itself. Imagery: white scales under moonlight, a river that
has been paved over and still runs beneath, lanterns moving on dark
water, the hush before a storm.
